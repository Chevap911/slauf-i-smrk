# Character Reference Notes

## Character 1: Šlauf (Bald with Glasses)
- **Build**: Short, stocky/chubby, cartoon proportions (large head, small body)
- **Head**: Completely bald, large round head
- **Face**: Round face, big blue eyes, round black-framed glasses, small blue/dark mustache, rosy/pink nose, thick dark eyebrows, friendly expression
- **Outfit**: Teal/dark blue denim overalls over a yellow/khaki long-sleeve shirt, yellow rubber gloves, yellow rubber boots with buckle straps
- **Style**: 3D Pixar/animated style, highly detailed textures

## Character 2: Šmrk (Bearded with Hair)
- **Build**: Short, stocky, cartoon proportions (large head, small body), slightly taller/leaner than Šlauf
- **Head**: Messy/tousled dirty blonde/golden brown hair
- **Face**: Full bushy brown/golden beard, big blue-green/teal eyes, rosy/pink nose, thick eyebrows, friendly expression
- **Outfit**: Teal/dark blue denim overalls over a bright yellow/golden long-sleeve shirt, blue/teal rubber gloves, dark boots with buckle straps
- **Style**: 3D Pixar/animated style, highly detailed textures
